# What's for Dinner?

Turn dinner indecision into a playful food lottery. Press OK, watch categories
and dishes roll past, and let a choice from 60 foods across 12 categories settle
tonight's question.

![AI-generated gameplay illustration](assets/images/tonight-dinner-cover.png)

The cover is an AI-generated gameplay illustration, not a device screenshot.

## How to play

- Use Up and Down to select an option; press OK to confirm and hold OK to go back.
- Start a draw, choose “That's the one!”, or try again. Repeated rerolls uncover
  little jokes for the wonderfully indecisive.
- Choose Anything, Budget, Light, Bold or Group mode. Narrow your choices by
  spending level, foods you avoid and dislikes; your settings are saved.
- Fries, a smiling burger, a drink and a crowned snowman cheer you on.

Works entirely offline. Read the [complete play guide](docs/dinner.md) for controls
and the [validation report](docs/dinner-validation.md) for tested behavior.

## Firmware and source

The initial version is `1.1.0-dinner`. See the [release notes](docs/dinner-release.md)
for firmware verification, installation and build instructions.

Based on the official [FoloToy AI Passport](https://github.com/FoloToy/ai-passport)
project. The application implements its own dinner lottery interface.
